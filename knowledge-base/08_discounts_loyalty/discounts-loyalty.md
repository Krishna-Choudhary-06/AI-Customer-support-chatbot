## Coupon troubleshooting

1. Ask which offer is being applied and where the customer sees the issue. Do not request account secrets.
2. Check the offer's approved terms: dates/timezone, eligible region, products, seller, minimum spend, customer eligibility, usage count, and stacking rules.
3. Compare the cart's eligible subtotal against the terms and verify the checkout calculation.
4. Explain the specific documented reason only. If eligibility or calculation cannot be verified, escalate rather than guessing.
5. Do not promise retroactive credit unless an approved rule and authorized action confirm it.
