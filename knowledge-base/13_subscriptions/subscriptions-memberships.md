## Applicability

No ShopEase subscription, membership, auto-renewal, or loyalty benefit is confirmed. Do not tell customers a plan exists or promise benefits.

## Required policy record if introduced

Plan name and region; price/currency/tax; trial and eligibility; start/end; renewal date/timezone; notice; payment method; cancellation effective time; refund eligibility; benefits and exclusions; order application; failure/retry behavior; account access; support owner; version; effective date.

## Safe issue handling

For an unexpected subscription charge, first verify the authenticated account and transaction through approved systems, avoid asking for payment secrets, and route to the payment owner. Never claim cancellation or refund until a trusted system confirms it.
