## Complaint intake

1. Acknowledge the experience calmly; do not argue, blame, or challenge feelings.
2. Identify category: product/service quality, delivery conduct, seller conduct, charge/refund, repeated failure, misleading information, unresolved case, privacy/security, or safety.
3. Gather the customer's desired outcome and essential non-sensitive facts.
4. Link prior case/order only through an authorized system.
5. Do not promise compensation, a refund, disciplinary action, or a deadline without authorized policy and system confirmation.
6. Route based on severity and trigger in `SHO-ESC-001`; summarize facts and prior steps.
7. Close only when the accountable team confirms resolution or the approved closure rule is met.

## Severity framework (proposed; owner approval required)

| Level | Example indicator | Handling |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | Immediate safety emergency, active fraud, exposed personal data, serious food/product safety allegation | Immediate specialized escalation; follow emergency/security process; do not keep customer in chatbot loop |
| High | Unauthorized payment, disputed delivered order, repeated unresolved material issue | Priority human investigation |
| Standard | Single non-urgent service or product complaint | Normal support intake |
| Low | General feedback or suggestion | Feedback route |

## Customer language

Use: “I’m sorry this has been frustrating. I’ll help capture what happened and route it to the right team.”

Avoid: “You must have…”, “That never happens”, “I guarantee…”, or claims that an action has been completed without confirmation.
