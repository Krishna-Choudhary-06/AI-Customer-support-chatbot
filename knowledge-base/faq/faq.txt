COMPANY: ShopEase (fictional demo business)
CATEGORY: General FAQs

Q1: How do I create an account?

A: Visit the registration page. Enter your name, email address, and password. Submit the form to create your account.

Q2: How can I contact customer support?

A: Customers can contact the ShopEase support team through the support ticket system.

Q3: How do I reset my password?

A: Visit the login page and select the Forgot Password option.

Q4: Can I change my registered email?

A: Customers must contact ShopEase support to request a registered email address change.
