## Non-negotiable chatbot controls

- Never request passwords, OTPs, PINs, recovery codes, full card numbers, CVV, or banking passwords.
- Never disclose another person's account, address, order, payment, or case information.
- Do not reveal internal fraud signals, detection thresholds, security tooling, or investigation methods.
- Do not ask customers to send identity documents or sensitive screenshots in ordinary chat; use only the approved secure workflow.
- Collect the minimum personal data required for the stated support task.
- Treat suspected account takeover, unauthorized payment, phishing, data exposure, or privacy-rights request as human-specialist work.

## Customer report: suspected fraud or unauthorized transaction

1. Acknowledge the report and advise the customer to use their bank/payment provider's official contact method where immediate payment protection is needed.
2. Do not ask them to share authentication codes or complete credentials.
3. Capture only approved non-sensitive transaction references and time.
4. Route immediately to the designated security/payment team.
5. Do not promise reversal, reimbursement, account recovery, or investigation outcome.
