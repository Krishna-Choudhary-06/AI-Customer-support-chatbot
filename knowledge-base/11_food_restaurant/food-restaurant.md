## Applicability

ShopEase is currently described as an online store for electronics, clothing, accessories, and home products. No food, restaurant, ingredient, allergen, preparation, or restaurant-partner service is established. This module must remain disabled unless the business owner confirms a food-delivery or restaurant model.

## Safety rules if enabled

- Use verified restaurant/menu ingredient and allergen data only.
- Never guarantee allergen absence, cross-contamination absence, nutritional effect, or medical safety without approved evidence.
- For a suspected contamination, illness, foreign object, severe allergic reaction, or immediate danger, prioritize customer safety and immediate human/emergency escalation according to local approved procedure.
- Do not diagnose or give medical advice.
- Preserve order facts and route food safety concerns to the designated safety owner.
