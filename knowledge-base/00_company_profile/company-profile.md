## Trust and publication rules

1. Treat all existing ShopEase facts as fictional demo data, not a real company's commitments.
2. Do not expose a `[COMPANY INPUT REQUIRED]` value as an answer. Ask for clarification or use the approved unknown-information response.
3. Before a demo policy is used as a business rule, have the fictional business owner define eligibility, exclusions, evidence, start/end points, exceptions, and effective dates.
4. Do not turn recommendations or workflow examples in this repository into customer-facing promises.
5. Keep internal-only procedures separate from customer-facing answers.

## Business model selection

Current configuration selects an **online-store learning scenario** only. The project does not establish whether ShopEase is a marketplace, the seller of record, or a single-brand retailer. Seller, restaurant, food, subscription, and loyalty procedures remain out of scope until the model is selected.
